## Product goal

Build one local-first JARVIS executive for the owner's Mac. The owner states a goal; JARVIS interprets it, proposes a plan for nontrivial work, obtains the required approval, chooses existing capabilities, executes, observes progress, independently verifies effects, and reports concise results. OpenJarvis is a candidate agent runtime, not a replacement product or a complete assistant by itself. The JARVIS app, executive service, policy, task store, adapters and verifier remain responsible for the product and its controls.
